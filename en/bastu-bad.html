Relaxation at the edge of the forest

A glowing calm under the starry sky.

Let the silence of the forest embrace you. At Pinebo Palace a wood-fired sauna and an inviting, warm hot tub await – available to you during your stay. The perfect way to unwind even further.

01 · The traditional wood fire

The wood-fired forest sauna

The scent of burning birch wood and the gentle crackle of the stove set the tone straight away. The sauna is built in classic Nordic style, and after the heat you cool off in the cold shower. This is where you truly unwind.

  • Room for up to 4 peoplePlenty of room for you and your companions – the guesthouse sleeps up to 4 guests.
  • Light the fire yourselfEnjoy the satisfaction of lighting the stove yourself. All firewood is included in your booking.
  • Heat your wayYou control the temperature – from mild, gentle warmth to intense, deep heat.
02 · Always ready

Hot tub on the deck under the open sky

Slip into the steaming, 38-degree water after a long day of hiking, or simply to end the evening in the best possible way. The hot tub stands on the deck right outside the guesthouse, sheltered by a wooden privacy screen – with the treetops and the starry sky above you.

  • Always warm and invitingThe hot tub stays at a constant, comfortable 38 °C throughout your stay.
  • Crystal-clear waterEco-friendly treated water that is gentle on the skin and free from harsh chemicals.
  • Atmospheric lightingSubtle LED lighting in soft colours enhances the magic of the night.
How it works

The easy way to unwind

The sauna and hot tub experience at Pinebo Palace is designed to be simple and deeply enjoyable from start to finish.

  1. 1

    Preparation

    When you arrive, the stove is ready with dry birch wood. The hot tub is already heated to a perfect 38 degrees.

  2. 2

    Heating up

    Light the sauna stove at your own pace. After about 45 minutes the temperature is just right and the scent of the forest fills the air.

  3. 3

    Cool down

    Cool off in the cold shower or the fresh air on the deck – then slip into the warm hot tub under the stars.

Frequently asked questions

Practical information for your visit

Here are the most common questions about the sauna and hot tub. If you can't find the answer you're looking for, you're always welcome to contact us directly.

Are towels and bathrobes included?

You can bring your own towels or add our bed linen & towels package (250 SEK) when you book. It includes large, soft bath sheets too.

How much firewood is included for the sauna?

Plenty of dry birch firewood is always included for your whole booking. Should you need more, there is an extra supply on the property at no extra cost.

How do I book the extras and what do they cost?

Simply add the sauna (400 SEK/day) and hot tub (from 400 SEK) when you choose your dates in our booking. If you combine both, a package discount of 10 %.

Do I share the sauna and hot tub with other guests?

No. Pinebo Palace is let to one party at a time, so the sauna and hot tub you book are just for you. We hosts live in our own house on the same farm, but the guesthouse and deck are secluded and face away from our home.

Is there a shower after the sauna?

Yes – cool off in the cold shower after the sauna. Many guests find it especially lovely after a day of hiking.
