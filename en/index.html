Pinebo Palace – close to nature

Switch off to reconnect with what really matters.

Pinebo Palace is a cosy guesthouse on our farm in the forest outside Ulricehamn, right beside a babbling stream. Wake up to birdsong and the sound of the water, enjoy your morning coffee on the deck and let the days take as long as they need.

The guesthouse is secluded, with its own entrance and a deck facing away from our home – so you can relax in peace and quiet. You can also find us on Airbnb, but here you book directly with us: no platform fees and personal contact all the way.

Frequently asked questions

Frequently asked questions about staying in Ulricehamn

How do check-in and check-out work?

Check-in is from 15:00 and check-out is by 11:00. We love to welcome you in person when we can – otherwise you check in easily yourself using the key box.

Can I bring my dog?

Yes, dogs are very welcome! The deck around the guesthouse is fenced, so your dog can be off the lead there. On the farm, dogs must be on a lead, and we pick up after them. Unfortunately we cannot accept cats due to allergies.

Do the hosts live on the farm?

Yes, we live in our own house on the same farm. The guesthouse is secluded, with its own entrance and a deck facing away from our home, so you can enjoy your stay in peace and quiet. Our dogs Bentley and Vera also live on the farm.

Do I need to bring bed linen and towels?

You can bring your own, or rent bed linen, towels and bath sheets from us for 250 SEK. Just add it when you choose your extras.

Are there hiking trails nearby?

Yes, there are many lovely hiking trails within 20 minutes by car. And the forest starts right at the deck. In the guesthouse you'll find a house manual and a QR code with our best tips for the area.

Are there supermarkets nearby?

Yes, there are several supermarkets in Ulricehamn, a short drive from the cabin.

What if I need to cancel?

You can cancel free of charge up to 14 days before arrival.

Do I have to clean myself?

Either follow our simple cleaning checklist, or add final cleaning for 800 SEK and we'll take care of it.
