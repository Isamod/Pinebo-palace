Discover every corner of Pinebo Palace

Ulricehamn, Västergötland

A retreat where nature sets the pace

Pinebo Palace is a cosy guesthouse on our farm in the forest outside Ulricehamn, right beside a babbling stream. The guesthouse is secluded, with its own entrance and a deck facing away from our home. Start the morning with freshly brewed coffee on the deck, step straight out into the mossy forest and end the evening in the wood-fired sauna or the warm hot tub.
